The Science

About Our Methodology

The Feynman Technique is a mental model that forces you to simplify complex concepts. By teaching a concept to our AI, you reveal gaps in your knowledge quickly. If you can't explain it simply, you haven't mastered it yet.

Our AI maps the relationships between various concepts as nodes in a graph. This visualization helps you see the bigger picture and how different topics interrelate, eliminating the feeling of learning in silos.
